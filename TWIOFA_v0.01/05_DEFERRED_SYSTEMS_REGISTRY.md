# Deferred Systems Registry

These systems are intentionally deferred until the substrate proof is validated.

## High Priority After Success
- Sparse chunk paging and residency management
- Larger explorable yard
- Improved GPU scheduling and workgroup optimization
- Richer ant perception and decision making
- Minimal field notes / observation UI

## Medium Priority
- Procedural yard elements (roots, rocks, food sources)
- Multi-channel scent fields
- Basic save/load of the single chunk state
- Simple collapse propagation across larger areas

## Low Priority (Post-Proof)
- Full colony simulation and economy
- Brood / reproduction systems
- Combat or threat response AI
- Weather or seasonal effects
- Modding or content authoring pipeline

These are not rejected — they are simply not relevant until the core material interaction loop proves itself.
