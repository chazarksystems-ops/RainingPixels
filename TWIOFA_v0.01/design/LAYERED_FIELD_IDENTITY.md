# Layered Field Identity — TWIOFA

TWIOFA is built around three distinct but connected layers of play. These layers are not just camera modes; they have different affordances, risks, and gameplay focuses.

## Upper Layer
- Provides broad context and yard orientation.
- More vertical traversal and canopy/surface elements.
- Less central to the core material-digging and collapse gameplay in v0.
- Serves as orientation and occasional vertical movement rather than the primary substrate interaction layer.

## Ground / Open Yard Layer
- The main tactical traversal and surface interaction space.
- Visible surface routes, food sources, hazards, and points of interest.
- World-click interaction feels most direct here.
- Material consequence exists but is often more horizontal and route-oriented than structural collapse.

## Underground Layer
- The primary **material substrate** layer for the first proof.
- Focuses on tunnels, moisture, structural integrity, collapse risk, and route liability.
- This is where digging has the strongest and most visible physical consequences.
- Ants are most vulnerable here (nest safety, route blockage, flooding/collapse).
- The substrate proof should prioritize making underground material consequence feel meaningful and dangerous.

## Implications for the Substrate Proof

While the first proof may focus primarily on underground material interaction (digging, moisture, collapse), it must not erase the larger three-layer identity of the game. Future slices should preserve different mechanical and visual affordances per layer instead of collapsing everything into one flat board experience.

The proof should make it clear that **underground material consequence is the first and most critical target**, while still acknowledging that the full game will involve meaningful play across all three layers.
