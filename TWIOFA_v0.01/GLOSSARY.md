# GLOSSARY.md — TWIOFA Foundation Terms

## TWIOFA_SUBSTRATE_PROOF_v0

The first narrow proof that ant-scale material interaction can be the core of the game.

## Substrate

The living material world: soil, water, moisture, collapse, tunnels, residue, and other cell-based material state.

## Substrate-Proof-First

The development rule that TWIOFA must prove material interaction before building broad infrastructure.

## One Active Chunk

A fixed simulation area used to prove the material loop before sparse streaming or infinite world systems exist.

## Packed u32 Cell State

A 32-bit cell representation containing material id, flags, scalar/moisture/cohesion, and aux/scent data.

## CA

Cellular automata: local cell-state rules that update material behavior.

## LBM

Lattice Boltzmann Method. Rejected for v0 because TWIOFA needs ant-scale mud/seepage/collapse gameplay, not full fluid solver complexity.

## World-Click Visible Execution

Player acts in the world, ants physically execute, and the world visibly changes.

## Noita-Floor

A shorthand visual target: material consequence should feel like continuous simulated matter as a minimum direction, not like a debug tile board. It is a direction marker, not a requirement to clone Noita.

## Tunnel Liability

The idea that tunnels are useful but dangerous; bad digging can trigger collapse, flooding, or route failure.

## Layered Field Identity

The distinction between upper, ground/open yard, and underground views. The substrate proof focuses underground first but should not erase the full layered game shape.
