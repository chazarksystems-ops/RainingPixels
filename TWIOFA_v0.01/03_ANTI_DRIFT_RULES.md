# Anti-Drift Rules for TWIOFA_SUBSTRATE_PROOF_v0

These rules are non-negotiable during the substrate proof phase.

## Forbidden Framing

- Do not call this a finished engine or complete runtime.
- Do not treat compile success or visual polish as validation.
- Do not use the old flat tile prototype as the visual target.
- Do not describe success as "architecture complete."

## Forbidden Additions

Do **not** add during this phase:
- Sparse chunk streaming or infinite yard
- Procedural generation
- LBM, Navier-Stokes, or advanced fluid solvers
- Temperature, gas, chemistry, or complex ecology
- Full ECS or multi-agent job systems
- Colony economy, brood, or caste systems
- Save/load pipeline
- Editor or modding tooling
- Networking or multiplayer
- Generic plugin / abstraction layers

## Required Language

Use these terms:
- Substrate proof
- Material interaction proof
- Single-chunk vertical slice
- Living material yard
- Visible consequence loop

Avoid these terms:
- Full engine
- Final renderer
- Complete simulation
- Infinite world
- Production system

## Visual Anti-Drift Rule

The default rendering **must not** read primarily as a clean square tile grid. Material continuity, wetness distinction, disturbance, and collapse must be visually prioritized over grid structure. A debug grid overlay may exist but must be off by default.
