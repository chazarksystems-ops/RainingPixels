# Player-Visible Result

The player should see a single underground material scene containing ants, soil, water/moisture, and tunnelable terrain.

The player can issue a simple world-click/dig order.

Ants visibly move or attempt to move through the material space, dig or modify material, and encounter consequences from material state.

Success looks like:

- a tunnel opens because ants dig
- moisture or water visibly changes soil
- collapse or instability visibly affects a route
- ants visibly fail, reroute, or become blocked when terrain changes
- the world reads as material consequence, not a colored debug board

This player-visible result is more important than architectural completeness.
