# Acceptance Gates

This slice contract is accepted when it defines, and a later implementation can prove:

1. A world view opens and shows a material field.
2. The material field does not read primarily as a square tile debug board.
3. The player can issue a simple world-click or dig order.
4. Ants visibly respond.
5. Digging changes material.
6. Water/moisture visibly changes soil state.
7. Collapse or instability can alter a route.
8. Ants can fail, reroute, or become blocked due to terrain consequence.
9. The player can understand that soil/tunnels/routes matter.
10. No deferred large systems were added to fake progress.
