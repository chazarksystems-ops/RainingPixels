# Slice Intent: Surface / Ground / Underground View Identity

This slice documents the intended mechanical and visual differences between layers so that future development does not collapse TWIOFA into a single flat experience.

## Ground / Open Yard
- Primary tactical movement and surface interaction layer.
- World-click feels most direct.
- Routes, food, and hazards are more visible and horizontal.

## Underground
- Primary material consequence layer (digging, moisture, collapse, route liability).
- Structural risk and tunnel geometry matter most here.
- Ant vulnerability is highest.

## Upper
- Orientation and occasional vertical movement.
- Less central to core digging/collapse gameplay in early slices.

The substrate proof should make underground material consequence feel distinct and important without erasing the existence of the other layers.
